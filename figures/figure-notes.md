# Figure notes

All data-driven figures are generated by `make_figures.py` (run inside `figures/`);
the asserts in the script check the inequalities that each picture claims.
`fig_angular_deletion.tex` is drawn by hand.

| Figure | Label | Idea (one sentence) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fig_model.tex` | `fig:model` | Points live only on lines; disks connect across line intersections, while gaps along a line separate components. | Numerical illustration (seed 10, mu = 0.6, lambda = 1.4, r = 0.5, window [0,12]x[0,7]); components computed within the window. |
| `fig_angular_deletion.tex` | `fig:deletion` | Lines meeting two distant windows have normal angles in a short interval; deleting it makes the two restrictions independent. | Schematic; window 1 = [0,1]^2, window 2 = [5,6]x[0,1]; every retained line has slope of modulus > 1/4, every deleted line < 1/4. |
| `fig_polygon.tex` | `fig:polygon` | Four lines enclose Q_L inside the annulus Q_{1.6L} \ Q_{1.3L}; dense points on the boundary block closed crossings and make the tiles along Gamma an open ring. | Exact construction for L = r = 1, h = 1/8, one sampled point per partition interval (event H); slopes enlarged to <= 1/20 (inclusions Q_{1.3L} in int P, P in Q_{1.6L} re-checked by assert). |
| `fig_recursion.tex` | `fig:recursion` | A crossing at scale R = aL contains two crossings at scale L centred on L Z^2 at distance >= (a-1)L. | Schematic path, a = 6 instead of a >= 2^20; u on boundary of Q_R, v on boundary of Q_{2R}, rounding and first-exit subpaths computed by the script. |
| `fig_boundary.tex` | `fig:boundary` | The exterior boundary T of a finite open cluster S is closed, star-connected, and conv S is contained in conv T. | Exact for the hand-chosen S (h = 1); U, T, holes, hulls computed; nearest-neighbour connectedness of S and star-connectedness of T asserted. |

Open points: independent review of the figures by a second reader has not been done; author approval pending.
