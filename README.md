# Isotropic Poisson line Cox percolation

Christian Hirsch (Göttingen) and Benedikt Jahnel (TU Braunschweig / WIAS Berlin)

Working notes on the planar Boolean model with disks of radius $r$ centred at the points of a Poisson line Cox process: Poisson points of linear intensity $\lambda$ on the lines of an isotropic Poisson line process of intensity $\mu$.

## Main results

* **Supercritical phase (Theorem 2.1 of the main note).** For every $\mu,r>0$ there is a universal constant $C$ with $\lambda_c(\mu,r)\le Cr^{-1}[1+\log^+(1/(\mu r))]$.
* **No exponential decay (Proposition 4.1 of the subcritical note).** For every $\lambda,\mu,r>0$, the probability of a connection over distance $n$ is at least $\exp(-(4+o(1))(\log n)^2/(\lambda r))$.
* **Quenched path criterion (Theorem 5.1 of the subcritical note).** $\lambda_c(\mu,r)>0$ follows from linear growth of the maximal sum of line counts along lattice paths, a statement about the line process alone (Conjecture 7.2, open).

## Open problem

Positivity of $\lambda_c(\mu,r)$, i.e. existence of a subcritical phase, raised in Hilário, Sá, Sanchis, Teixeira (PTRF 2026, Section 9) and in Jahnel, Jhawar, Vu (EJP 2023). For the current state start with `subcritical-phase/REVISION.txt` and `subcritical-phase/analysis/00-summary.md`; the reference document is `subcritical-phase/referee/main.pdf`, which collects everything that is proved with full proofs and states the open steps as boxed claims (D, C1–C3), and which corrects five errors in the earlier notes (chain definition and thickness definition in the first-moment note, where hypothesis (T) was false as stated; the naive dependence hypothesis, false because of relays; separation of sub-boxes and closure of the weight under shared lines in the multiscale note); the decorations note explains why the cover-based route cannot close by itself.

**Corrections.** The revision of 3 November 2026 withdrew two claims of the earlier greedy-animals note: the exponential moment of the reward along a straight finite path is finite (it was claimed infinite), and the earlier "highway obstruction" to the dyadic scheme rested on an interval rate $\mu/t$ that should be $\mu/t^2$; the corrected calculation gives an exponential upper bound for that mechanism. The obstruction that remains is the oscillating-corridor lower bound. Statements withdrawn in earlier files are marked there.
